3 Places as a first-class thing, real add and delete, glass surfaces
The yard is gone; life and errands take the place-locked section over entirely. Places are their own list — town, supermarket, church, gym, your parents’, anywhere — and you add to it. Items that have a time carry a countdown to the moment they go live (Sunday service, 09:00 Sun, 23 min from now) and every place group is sorted soonest first; items with no set time sink to the bottom. Capture takes a title, a rule, a stream and a place, and every row has a delete. Cards are frosted and lift under the cursor, the cursor leaves a trail, buttons buzz on devices that support it, and the bell raises real system notifications once you allow them. Two honest limits are at the bottom of this page.
Seven things were added on top: weekly repeats you can skip once or end outright, travel time and a one-trip bundle for errands, a Zones tab that lays every deadline on your own working day, a week review with a completion streak, a proposed new slot for anything you missed, a focus timer that hides the rest of the app, and a receipt you can confirm on work someone else set.
The icon is the model: three lanes of different lengths, one per governing rule — clock-locked, place-locked, self-driven — with a place marker sitting off to the side. It holds at 26px because the shapes differ in length and colour, not in detail.

Two honest limits Notifications here are local: the bell requests real system permission and raises real notifications, but only while this page is open. Alerts that reach a phone with the app closed need a service worker and a push server behind it, which a design prototype has no way to stand up. Haptics use the standard vibration API, which Android supports and iOS Safari does not — on an iPhone the buttons will simply stay silent.
